## Overview
A production-ready framework for Large Language Model (LLM) alignment, Chain-of-Thought (CoT) evaluation, bias screening, and automated benchmark scoring.

## Core Features
- **Chain-of-Thought Rubric Scoring:** Standardized evaluation of multi-step logical reasoning across complex prompts.
- **Automated Bias & Policy Screening:** Heuristic and regex pipeline flagging policy-inconsistent model outputs.
- **Side-by-Side Model Ranking:** Pairwise preference evaluation utility for model comparison.
